# Phase 1 Initial Technical Risk Register

| Risk ID | Asset | Threat | Vulnerability / Failure | Impact | Likelihood | Risk rating | Existing / proposed control | Detection | Remediation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | PHI object storage | External attacker | Public exposure or weak access boundary | High | Medium | High | No public access, private access model, encryption, discovery tooling | Storage exposure findings, access logs | Lock down exposure, validate encryption, recheck audit path | Exposure evidence, remediation proof |
| R-02 | Workload identity | Privilege abuse | Over-broad permissions | High | Medium | High | Least privilege, separation of duties, access review | Suspicious API activity, authorization review | Reduce permissions, rotate creds, revalidate | IAM review records, validation evidence |
| R-03 | KMS domain | Misconfiguration | Application loses decrypt ability or key scope is too broad | High | Medium | High | Separate key domains and admin/use separation | KMS failure telemetry, app symptoms | Correct policy/binding, retest access | Failure and recovery evidence |
| R-04 | Database tier | External or insider access | Public exposure or unauthorized connectivity | High | Low | High | Private network design, app-mediated access only | Exposure findings, network telemetry | Close exposure, validate private path | Network validation evidence |
| R-05 | WAF / edge | External attacker | Filtering gaps or bypass path | Medium | Medium | Medium | WAF plus app-layer defenses | WAF logs, attack simulation results | Tune policy, fix upstream validation | Block/allow evidence |
| R-06 | Audit plane | Insider / admin misuse | Logging disabled, incomplete, or bypassed | High | Medium | High | Mandatory infra + app auditing, protected log destinations | Missing log detection, config alerts | Restore logging, investigate cause, validate telemetry | Logging failure evidence |
| R-07 | Identity plane | Credential compromise | Stolen workforce or admin credentials | High | Medium | High | MFA, narrow privileges, monitoring | Sign-in anomalies, suspicious API use | Revoke sessions, rotate credentials, tighten controls | Identity and response evidence |
| R-08 | Application authorization | Insider or attacker | Broken object-level authorization / IDOR | High | Medium | High | Record-level authorization and app audit events | App audit anomalies, test validation | Fix authz logic, revalidate access controls | Validation and remediation evidence |
| R-09 | Backup plane | Destructive actor | Backup deletion or unusable backups | High | Medium | High | Backup isolation, encryption, retention, restore tests | Backup failure alerts, restore tests | Harden access, recover, retest | Backup and recovery evidence |
| R-10 | IaC / config plane | Insider / CI-CD misuse | Drift or unreviewed configuration change | Medium | Medium | Medium | Desired-state authority, drift detection, change review | Drift signals, change audit | Reconcile to approved state, improve controls | Drift investigation evidence |
| R-11 | Security plane | Cross-cloud attacker | AWS/GCP credential reuse or blast-radius expansion | High | Low | Medium | Credential separation and domain isolation | Cross-cloud access anomalies | Revoke and segregate credentials, revalidate isolation | Access evidence and review notes |
| R-12 | Logs and telemetry | Sensitive data leakage | Unnecessary patient detail in logs | High | Medium | High | Logging restrictions, redaction strategy, minimal payload logging | Log review, sensitive-data discovery | Sanitize logs, adjust schemas, revalidate | Log-review evidence |
